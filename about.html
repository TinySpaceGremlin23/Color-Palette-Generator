Hey, look. Another color generator thing.
I'm not the best at explaining things, but the palettes are made
using the position of the letters of whatever the input is,
ASCII Codes and some math.

You start with the ASCII value of the letter.
Here's an entire guide to ASCII, all the way down to the chart.

After that, you add that number to the number the letter's
position is (the "name.length" in the JS.) The "index" always starts at 0,
so in the video, the position of "C" is 0. You then multiply that by 32
(right now that's a placeholder to give the palettes more range
so I might change it.)

After that, you get your seed, which you then do the math with the multipliers (123,456,789). The % is modulus, or "what remains after perfect divison".
256 is the number of shades for the RGB channels. Here's a site for that
