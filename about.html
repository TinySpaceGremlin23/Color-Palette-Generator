Hey, look. Another color generator thing.
Not the best at explaining, but the palettes
are made using the position of the letters of
the input, ASCII Codes and some math.

You start with the ASCII value of the letter.
Here's an entire guide for that,
all the way down to the chart.

After that, add the number to the number the letter's position
(the "name.length" in the JS.) The "index" always starts at 0, so
in the video, the position of "C" is 0. You then multiply that
by 72, the number I set to gives the palettes some range.

You get your "seed", then do the math with
the multipliers (123,456,789). The % is modulus,
or "what remains after perfect divison".
256 is the number of shades for the RGB
channels Here's a site for all that stuff.
